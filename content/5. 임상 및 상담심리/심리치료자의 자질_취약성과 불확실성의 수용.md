>If you don't like vulnerability, you probably shouldn't become a therapist. I would say, if you don't like uncertainty, you probably shouldn't become a therapist. Because so many curveballs come in the door every day. And I think for me, that's exciting. That's what makes it interesting that you never know, no matter how well you think you know a person, you really don't know what they're going to say that day or what they're going to share, What you're going to learn about them. And also, if you don't like making mistakes, don't become a therapist. 취약성을 좋아하지 않는다면 치료사가 되어서는 안 될 것입니다. 불확실성을 좋아하지 않는다면 치료사가 되어서는 안 된다고 말하고 싶습니다. 매일 수많은 커브볼이 날아오니까요. 저는 그런 점이 흥미롭다고 생각해요. 아무리 사람을 잘 안다고 생각해도 그 사람이 그날 무슨 말을 할지, 어떤 이야기를 나눌지, 그 사람에 대해 무엇을 알게 될지 알 수 없다는 점이 흥미롭습니다. 또한 실수하는 것을 좋아하지 않는다면 치료사가 되지 마세요. by 로리 고틀립
